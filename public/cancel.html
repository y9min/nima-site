Cancel or change your subscription

Cancellation steps depend on where your subscription started.

How to tell where you subscribed

If nima appears in your Apple ID subscriptions, cancel through Apple. If it appears in Google Play subscriptions when Android is available, cancel through Google Play. If your receipt came from nima or another payment provider, use the direct path on Manage.

iPhone

App Store

Open Settings, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select nima, then choose Cancel Subscription.

Android

Google Play

When Android is available, open Google Play, tap your profile, choose Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, nima, then Cancel.

Direct

Direct billing

Use the Manage page with the email from your receipt to find the right next step.

Refunds

Apple handles App Store refund requests. Google handles Google Play refund requests when Android is available. Direct purchases, if offered, should be raised through Contact with the email used at checkout.

After cancellation

You may keep access until the end of the paid billing period. Deleting the app does not cancel billing, so use the correct cancellation path before your renewal date.

Need the right link?

Start with Manage if you are unsure which billing path applies.
