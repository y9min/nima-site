Help

Practical answers for setting up nima and keeping your limits working.

Getting started

  1. Open nima and choose the feeds or apps you want to limit.
  2. Set daily limits that feel realistic enough to keep.
  3. Keep required permissions turned on so nima can apply limits.
  4. Check your progress and streaks to see how your habits are changing.

Blocking or limits are not working

Check that nima has the required permissions, reopen the app, restart your device, and update both nima and your operating system. If you use other screen-time tools, make sure their settings do not conflict.

Progress looks wrong

Progress and streaks can depend on device settings, permissions, and when the app last synced activity. If something looks delayed, reopen nima and check your permissions.
