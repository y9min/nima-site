Privacy Policy

Nima respects your privacy. This policy explains what data Nima collects, why it is used, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how you can request deletion.

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Version: 1.0

If you have questions or privacy requests, contact us at help@nima.so.

1. Who We Are

Nima is an app and service that helps people reduce distracting app use through account features, subscriptions, VPN-based filtering, traffic dashboards, and related backend services.

This policy applies to the Nima iOS app, web dashboard, Android waitlist, VPN/filtering backend, account systems, subscription systems, and support flows.

2. Data We Collect

Nima may collect the following data:

Account data: Email address, Supabase user ID, authentication data, session data, Apple Sign In data, and Google Sign-In data.

Purchase data: Subscription status, purchase history, entitlement state, RevenueCat app user ID, purchase data, restore data, and offering information.

VPN and filtering data: Hostnames, SNI/domain names, ports, URL paths where server proxy logging is enabled, request method, status code, content type, bytes in and out, request duration, block or allow decision, block reason, app category, VPN client IP, and user ID.

Usage analytics: App or category request counts, blocked and allowed counts, top domains, bandwidth, methods, content types, dashboard summaries, and generated insights.

Cancellation feedback: Selected cancellation reason and optional free-text details.

Android waitlist data: Email address and signup timestamp.

Diagnostics: App and tunnel logs, VPN lifecycle state, connection errors, performance counters, crash-like or stop diagnostics, local traffic dashboard files, and related troubleshooting data.

Local-only app data: Onboarding answers, age, phone-hours answer, selected habits/apps, display name, streaks, reminder settings, time windows, notification preferences, and CoreMotion data used for visual motion effects.

3. How We Use Data

Nima uses data to:

  • Provide account login and session management.
  • Operate VPN and content blocking features.
  • Classify traffic and decide whether requests should be allowed or blocked.
  • Show traffic dashboards, app usage summaries, blocking history, bandwidth information, and insights.
  • Process subscriptions, purchases, entitlements, and purchase restores.
  • Send Android waitlist updates and service communications.
  • Handle cancellation feedback, account deletion, billing issues, and customer support.
  • Debug reliability, prevent abuse, improve app functionality, and maintain security.

4. Third-Party Processors

Nima uses service providers to operate the app and service.

Supabase: Authentication, sessions, database storage, waitlist storage, account deletion support, traffic events, traffic summaries, feedback storage, and generated insight storage.

RevenueCat: Subscription status, purchase history, entitlements, offerings, purchases, and restores.

Google Sign-In: Google authentication data, user identifier, and IP address as described by Google.

Apple: Sign in with Apple and App Store purchase processing.

OpenAI: Hostname classification and aggregated traffic insight generation.

Hosting and infrastructure providers: Web app hosting, server infrastructure, database infrastructure, VPN/filtering operations, and related technical services.

5. AI Processing

Nima may send hostnames to OpenAI so they can be categorized into app or content categories.

Nima may send aggregated request counts, block counts, and usage summaries to OpenAI to generate short usage insights.

Nima does not intend to send full URL paths to OpenAI insight prompts. If this changes, this policy will be updated before the change is used.

OpenAI is used as a service provider for Nima functionality. Nima does not use OpenAI for advertising or cross-app tracking.

6. Retention

Account and authentication data is kept while your account exists.

Traffic events are kept by default for 30 days.

Aggregated summaries and generated insights are kept by default for 12 months.

Cancellation feedback is kept while your account exists or as needed for support, business records, or legal obligations.

Android waitlist emails are kept until Android launch communications are complete or until you ask us to remove your email.

Local diagnostic files and logs are kept on your device until rotated, cleared, or removed during account deletion or local reset.

Purchase records are kept as required by Apple, RevenueCat, tax rules, fraud prevention, and subscription operations.

When account deletion is available and requested, Nima deletes linked backend account data, traffic events, traffic summaries, generated insights, cancellation feedback, profile/blocker state, and local app-group data where available.

7. Sharing, Sale, and Tracking

Nima does not sell personal data.

Nima does not use personal data for third-party advertising.

Nima does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Nima does not use the App Tracking Transparency prompt because Nima does not track users across other companies' apps or websites.

Service providers process data only to provide Nima functionality, security, support, billing, analytics, AI features, or legal compliance.

8. Children

Nima is not directed to children under 13.

Users under 13 should not create an account or use the service.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You can request access, correction, deletion, or Android waitlist removal by emailing help@nima.so.

Where available, you can delete your account in the app.

You can disable local notifications in iOS settings or in Nima settings where supported.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when Nima changes or when legal, security, or operational requirements change.

The latest policy will be posted at https://nima.so/privacy-policy with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact help@nima.so.
