Support

Manage your subscription

The right place to manage nima depends on how you subscribed.

App Store

Manage with Apple

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your Apple ID, choose Subscriptions, then select nima.

Google Play

Manage with Google

When Android is available, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, and nima.

Direct

Request instructions

If you subscribed directly, email help@nima.so from the address on your receipt for management instructions.

Direct subscription

Find management instructions

Use the same email you used for checkout. This static site cannot look up your account here, so email help@nima.so for the correct support path.

Important

Deleting nima from your phone does not cancel billing. Cancel from the App Store, Google Play, or the direct subscription path before your renewal date.
