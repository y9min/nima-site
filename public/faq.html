FAQ

Short answers about using nima, managing subscriptions, and getting help.

What is nima?

nima is an app that helps you keep social media intentional by blocking addictive feeds, setting limits, and tracking progress.

How does nima help me use social media less?

It helps you create friction around the parts of social media that are easiest to overuse, then gives you feedback through limits, progress, and streaks.

Which apps or feeds can I limit?

Available options may depend on your device, operating system, and permissions. Check the app for the current set of supported limits.

Can I block short-form feeds?

nima is built around blocking addictive feeds, including short-form scrolling experiences where supported by your device and settings.

Is nima only for social media?

The main focus is social media habits. Some settings may help with broader screen-time routines, depending on what the app supports on your device.

Does nima track my progress?

Yes, nima includes progress tracking so you can see patterns and adjust your limits over time.

What are streaks?

Streaks show repeated progress over time. Treat them as feedback, not pressure.

Is Android available?

Android is coming soon. The homepage includes a Google Play waitlist form.

How do I join the Android waitlist?

Go back to the homepage and choose the Google Play button, then enter your email in the waitlist form.

How much does nima cost?

Pricing can vary by platform, region, trial, or offer. Check the App Store, Google Play when available, or checkout screen for current pricing.

How do I manage my subscription?

Use the Manage page to find the correct path for App Store, Google Play, or direct billing.

How do I cancel?

Visit Cancel for step-by-step guidance. The right steps depend on where you subscribed.

Does deleting the app cancel my subscription?

No. You need to cancel through Apple, Google Play when available, or the direct subscription path.

Why are limits or blocking not working?

Check permissions, update nima and your operating system, restart your device, and make sure other screen-time settings are not conflicting.

Why does my progress look delayed or inaccurate?

Progress can depend on permissions, device settings, and when nima last refreshed activity. Reopen the app and check permissions first.

Can I use nima across multiple devices?

Multi-device behavior depends on current app support, platform rules, and account setup. Check the app for the latest options.

How does nima handle privacy?

Privacy details belong in the Privacy Policy. Review that page for how data is handled.

How do I contact support?

Email help@nima.so or use the Contact page. Include your device, operating system, app version, and a clear description of the issue.
